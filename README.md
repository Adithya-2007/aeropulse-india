# AeroPulse India — National Environmental Telemetry Command Center

Interactive environmental intelligence and air quality telemetry platform covering all 36 Indian States and Union Territories (68 monitored hubs) with authentic CPCB NAQI 5-tier architecture, real-time Open-Meteo atmospheric streams, and Wikipedia municipal dossiers.

- **Live URL**: https://adithya-2007.github.io/aeropulse-india/
- **Baseline Scale**: Authentic CPCB NAQI (Good 0-50, Satisfactory 51-100, Moderate 101-200, Poor 201-300, Very Poor 301-400, Severe 401-500)
- **Geospatial Engine**: Google Maps (Roads, Hybrid Satellite, Topographic Relief) + Defined National & State Vector Outlines
- **Live APIs**:
  - Open-Meteo Air Quality API: https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/air-quality-api
  - Open-Meteo Weather & Wind Forecast API: https://open-meteo.com/en/docs
  - CPCB NAQI Official Standards: https://cpcb.nic.in/
  - Wikimedia REST API: https://en.wikipedia.org/api/rest_v1/
